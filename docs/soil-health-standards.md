# SoilPilot Soil Parameter Interpretation & Reference Standards

This document establishes the scientific reference standards, classification thresholds, and deterministic agronomic recommendations applied throughout the SoilPilot platform (including the Soil Health Card, Digital Soil Mapping interpretation, and Recommendations Engine).

## 2. Scientific References & Citations

- **R1**: **Soil Health Card (SHC) Scheme**, Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India. Diagnostic limits and general macronutrient adjustment factors.
- **R2**: **Indian Micronutrient Research (242,827 soil samples across 615 districts)**, Shukla, A. K., et al., ICAR–Indian Institute of Soil Science (IISS), Bhopal. All India Coordinated Research Project on Micro and Secondary Nutrients and Pollutant Elements in Soils and Plants (AICRP-MSPE).
- **R3**: **Soil Test Crop Response (STCR) & MPKV Rahuri STCR-IPNS Framework**, Mahatma Phule Krishi Vidyapeeth, Rahuri, Maharashtra. Targeted yield and integrated plant nutrition system equations for Maharashtra Vertisols and sugarcane cropping systems.
- **\*RDF**: **Recommended Dose of Fertilizers** (शिफारशीत खत मात्रा) as published by State Agricultural Universities (SAUs) for specific agro-ecological zones.

## 3. Parameter Grouping & Diagnostic Categories

### A. Chemical Reaction & Salinity (pH & EC)
- **Soil pH**: Governs nutrient availability and microbial activity. Acid soils (<6.0) fix phosphorus and calcium; alkaline soils (>7.5) induce zinc and iron deficiencies due to calcium carbonate precipitation.
- **Electrical Conductivity (EC)**: Measures total dissolved soluble salts. Values above 1.6 dS/m signify hazardous salinity requiring leaching, improved sub-surface drainage, and careful irrigation water management.

### B. Soil Organic Matter (OC)
- **Organic Carbon (OC)**: The primary determinant of soil biological health, cation exchange capacity (CEC), and water retention in Deccan black cotton soils (Vertisols). Maintaining OC > 0.75% is vital for sustained productivity.

### C. Primary Macronutrients (N, P, K)
- **Available Nitrogen (N)**: Classified as Low (<280 kg/ha), Medium (280–560 kg/ha), and High (>560 kg/ha). Low status triggers an upward adjustment to ~125% of RDF; high status justifies a reduction to ~75% of RDF to prevent lodging and groundwater leaching.
- **Available Phosphorus (P)**: Classified into Low (<10 kg/ha), Medium (10–25 kg/ha), High (25–50 kg/ha), and Very High (>50 kg/ha). Fields with >50 kg/ha should withhold phosphatic fertilizers to prevent zinc antagonism.
- **Available Potassium (K)**: Classified into Low (<120 kg/ha), Medium (120–280 kg/ha), High (280–600 kg/ha), and Very High (>600 kg/ha). Vertisols often have adequate to high potassium reserves; soils >600 kg/ha require no supplementary potassic fertilization.

### D. Secondary & Micronutrients (Fe, Zn, S, B, Cu, Mn)
- Evaluated via DTPA-extractable and 0.15% CaCl₂ extraction procedures.
- Low and deficient ratings trigger soil or foliar amendments (e.g., zinc sulphate, ferrous sulphate, elemental sulphur, or borax) strictly calibrated to prevent phytotoxicity.
